Simple Epidemic Model Simulators

  • SIR model
    Susceptible → Infected → Recovered. Compare the deterministic solution with stochastic replicates and their final size distribution.
  • SEIR model
    Adds an Exposed (latent) stage to the SIR model.
  • SIS model on contact networks
    Watch an infection spread and recover on real school and class contact networks, or a random network, compared with a well-mixed model.

The simulators run entirely in your browser. The first one you open takes a little while to load as it downloads R; after that they start faster.
